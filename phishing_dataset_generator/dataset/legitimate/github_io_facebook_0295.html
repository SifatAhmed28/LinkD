Facebook Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password" to receive a reset link via email.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Navigate to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup wizard.

How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team at support@facebook.com or through the in-app help widget.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data at rest and in transit.
